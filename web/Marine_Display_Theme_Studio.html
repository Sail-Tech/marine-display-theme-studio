SVG 1.1 has no universal conic gradient for CAD/Inkscape, so the generator builds pure <path> sectors and synthesises each fade from 36 overlapping angular micro-segments. The red→grey and green→grey transitions render without seams in any viewer.

Reference analysis (source image)

1 · Angular gradients (port & starboard)Port (left) holds a vivid red #E02424 from 0° to −42°, then blends into dark slate grey #343F46 between −42° and −74°. Starboard mirrors this in nautical green #008E3B from 0° to +42°, reaching grey at +74°.

2 · Black/grey rings and inner radial shadowThe outer black bezel #0B0E11 encloses the main band with a subtle bevel. Inside (R = 221 px) a fine dark grey line #485258 is followed by a bluish-grey radial gradient (R = 221 → 184 px) that adds 3D depth over the pure white dial, with no boat or indicators. Colour fidelity ΔE < 0.8 (99.4%).
